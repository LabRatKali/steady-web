What is this page / why /watch / what is a popunder?

Why there is /watch (and /testing)

Your live site is proxied by Cloudflare Worker → GitHub Pages. When I first fixed ads, /testing kept showing the old broken page (“frame blocked”) because Cloudflare cached it. Publishing again didn’t show up right away. I created /watch as a fresh path that wasn’t stuck in that cache so you could use the fixed lab immediately. Both /watch/ and /testing/ now serve this same improved lab.

Why ads looked broken before

1) Ads were loaded inside sandboxed iframes → every card said “frame blocked” (scripts couldn’t run).
2) Then the worker CDN kept serving that old HTML.
3) Even when fixed, Adsterra often leaves some slots empty (no fill for that size/moment). Empty ≠ broken — this lab retries empties.

What is a popunder?

A popunder is not a black box on this page. When Adsterra’s popunder script is armed, and you click once on the page, it tries to open the advertiser’s site in a new browser tab behind the one you’re using (under your current tab). That’s why it’s called pop-under, not popup.

You often won’t see anything happen on this page. Check your browser tab strip for a new tab, or look for a blocked-popup icon in the address bar. Chrome/Edge frequently block popunders. Use the green Arm & fire popunder card (real click required).

Smartlink

A smartlink is only a click URL — Adsterra does not draw a banner for it. This lab puts a real banner in that card and routes your click through the smartlink into the in-page browser below (or a new tab if you turn that off).
